How prices and combinations work

Combined odds multiply decimal prices across separate fights. One leg per fight. Totals are independent alternatives to your winner picks, not guarantees implied by them. A finish can happen before or after the total. Sourced sportsbook prices are multiplied across separate fights. Combined odds are calculated estimates, not a confirmed sportsbook quote or a guarantee these legs can be parlayed. Missing markets stay unpriced.
